## 📌 Executive Summary

Debuted under S2 Entertainment in July 2023, KISS OF LIFE built strong Western momentum due to vocal execution and members Julie and Belle growing up in the United States. However, an agency-sanctioned birthday livestream in April 2025 undid a significant share of international trust[cite: 8].

This case study breaks down:
* **The April 2025 Flashpoint:** An agency-organized broadcast featuring caricatured cultural tropes that triggered instant backlash across X, TikTok, and Reddit[cite: 8].
* **The Divergent Market Impact:** A 200,000+ Instagram unfollow drop and creator boycotts in Western markets contrasted against undisturbed domestic and Japanese streaming metrics[cite: 8].
* **The Deflection Loop:** An analysis of internal governance where leadership allowed an agency-approved concept to pass unreviewed, issued an evasive corporate statement, and directed members to write individual handwritten apology notes[cite: 8].
* **Strategic Takeaways:** Frameworks for cross-cultural pipeline reviews, executive accountability, and authentic genre engagement for global music acts[cite: 8].
